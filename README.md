# FMCG Sales, Marketing & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses FMCG sales and profitability data from 2023–2025 using **MySQL and Power BI**.

The analysis focuses on revenue performance, profitability, product categories, brands, regions, sales channels and monthly trends to identify patterns that can support data-driven business decisions.

## Tools Used

* **MySQL** — data analysis and SQL queries
* **Power BI** — interactive dashboard and data visualisation
* **DAX** — KPI calculations and measures

## Project Objectives

* Analyse overall sales and profit performance
* Compare revenue performance across regions
* Identify high-performing product categories and brands
* Analyse profitability across sales channels
* Track monthly revenue trends
* Calculate key business performance indicators

## Key Business Insights

* **Total Net Revenue:** $14.45M
* **Total Profit:** $3.31M
* **Total Units Sold:** 4M
* **Average Profit Margin:** 19.87%
* **Highest-performing region:** Europe — $4.81M net revenue
* **Highest-performing product category:** Beverages — $3.74M net revenue
* **Highest monthly net revenue:** December — $1.38M
* **Lowest monthly net revenue:** November — $1.14M
